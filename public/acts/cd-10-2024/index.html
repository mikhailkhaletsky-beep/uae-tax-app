Preamble and recitals

Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2024 Amending Certain Provisions of Cabinet Decision No. (75) of 2023 on the Administrative Penalties for Violations Related to the Application of Federal Decree-Law No. (47) of 2022 on the Taxation of Corporations and Businesses

The Cabinet:

-Having reviewed the Constitution,

-Federal Decree-Law No. (47) of 2022 on the Taxation of Corporations and Businesses, and its amendments,

-Cabinet Decision No. (75) of 2023 on the Administrative Penalties for Violations Related to the Application of Federal Decree-Law No. (47) of 2022 on the Taxation of Corporations and Businesses,

-Pursuant to what was presented by the Minister of Finance and the approval of the Cabinet,

Has decided:

#Article 1

The following shall be added to the Table of Violations and Administrative Penalties annexed to Cabinet Decision No. (75) of 2023, referred to above:

No. 14. Failure of the Taxable Person to submit the tax registration application within the period specified by the Authority in accordance with the Corporate Tax Law. Administrative penalty amount in AED: 10,000.

#Article 2

This Decision shall be published in the Official Gazette and shall come into effect on 1 March 2024.

Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum Prime Minister Issued by us: Date: 12 Shaban 1445 AH Corresponding to: 22 February 2024 AD

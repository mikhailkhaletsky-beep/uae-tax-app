About this database

UAE CT Database is a working title for a personal, non-commercial reference database of UAE corporate tax legislation. It holds the full texts of acts, not links to other websites.

What is in the database

  • Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 on the Taxation of Corporations and Businesses (consolidated text, with its earlier editions) and the amending Decree-Laws.
  • Cabinet Decisions made under the Law.
  • Ministerial Decisions of the Ministry of Finance made under the Law.
  • Federal Tax Authority (FTA) acts: FTA Decisions, the Corporate Tax Guides and Top-up Tax Guides, the Public Clarifications and the compilations of Private Clarifications.
  • Tax procedures: the Tax Procedures Law and its Executive Regulation.

Ministerial Decisions and FTA acts are added in English only, as published by the Ministry of Finance (mof.gov.ae) and the Federal Tax Authority (tax.gov.ae); each act page links to the publisher's original file, and the Arabic version, where it exists, is on the publisher's website. FTA Guides, Public Clarifications and Private Clarifications are guidance: they explain how the FTA applies the law, they are not legally binding and they do not change the law. A Public Clarification is addressed to all taxpayers; a Private Clarification is the FTA's answer to a question of one taxpayer, published in anonymised form in a compilation (“Summary of FTA Private Clarifications”), and is not binding on anyone else. Each compilation is a separate act; the topics of a compilation are listed under the articles of the Law that they cite (Related acts). The date shown for a guide or a clarification is its publication date (often only the month is known).

The FTA lists some documents on pages that could be read only in part, so the collection of FTA acts may not be complete. Gaps that are known are listed under Texts not yet loaded.

How the texts were prepared

  • English texts are taken from the publications of the Ministry of Finance (mof.gov.ae) and the Federal Tax Authority (tax.gov.ae). They are unofficial translations; the Arabic text prevails. For the Law, the amending Decree-Laws and the Cabinet Decisions the Arabic original is available on its own tab; Ministerial Decisions and FTA acts are loaded in English only.
  • Where no official English text exists, a machine translation is shown and labelled as such.
  • Issue and effective dates are taken from the text of each act, not from the dates shown in the portals' lists, which are often different.
  • Editions: the current consolidated text opens by default. Earlier editions of the Corporate Tax Law are listed in its version log, where they can be read and compared (legal redline). Next to each article that was amended, a faded link leads to the wording it had before.
  • The current consolidated text of the Corporate Tax Law is copied from the UAE Legislation portal (uaelegislation.gov.ae). Earlier editions are reconstructed from it by undoing the amending laws; the replaced articles come from the original English text published by the Ministry of Finance in 2022, so their wording can differ slightly from the rest of the text.
  • Terminology of the Corporate Tax Law is unified in every edition: the three variants “Supplementary Tax”, “Top-Up Tax” and “top-up tax” became “Top-up Tax”; “Decree by Law” became “Decree-Law”; “resolution” became “decision” (as in “decision issued by the Cabinet”); the term “Administrative Penalties” of the 2022 text became “Administrative Fines”; and wherever the text uses a term defined in Article 1 (Corporate Tax, Withholding Tax, Withholding Tax Credit, Foreign Tax Credit, Resident Person, Non-Resident Person, Permanent Establishment, Qualifying Mutual Fund, Multinational Enterprise, Administrative Fines) it is capitalised as in the definition. Nothing else in the wording was changed. The amending laws are shown as published.
  • Links between acts (for example from a Cabinet Decision to the article of the Law it implements) are generated automatically from the references in the text and should be checked.

Limits

This site is not legal advice and is not affiliated with any authority. Always check the official publication before relying on a text. Tables and figures in guides are shown in simplified form: check the PDF of the FTA for the exact layout.
